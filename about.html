Qualified from Masinde Muliro University of Science and Technology — I live at the crossroads of language, imagination, and social impact. My mission is to turn every learner into a critical thinker and compassionate storyteller.

My journey into words

Growing up in Kisumu, I discovered that stories hold the power to heal, challenge, and unite. This led me to pursue a Bachelor of Education (English & Literature) at Masinde Muliro University of Science and Technology, where I graduated with honors. For over 7 years, I have taught in diverse classrooms — from rural schools to international curricula — always championing the beauty of the English language and the depth of global literatures.

Beyond the classroom, I write books that explore identity, resilience, and the African narrative. I believe teaching is not a job; it's a calling. Every time a student unlocks a metaphor or finishes a poem they wrote, I see the future changing.
