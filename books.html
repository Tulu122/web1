✨ Published Works

📘🌿
Echoes of the Savanna
⭐ Literary Fiction · African Modern Classic

A lyrical novel that journeys through memory, migration, and the rhythms of rural Kenya. Follow young Adhiambo as she uncovers family secrets tied to the land and its oral traditions.

★★★★★ 4.8 · “A masterpiece” – Daily Nation Review
📖🍂
The Grammar of Belonging
🌟 Poetry · Contemporary Verse

An award-winning poetry collection exploring identity, displacement, and the healing power of language. Using English, Luo rhythms, and free verse.

★★★★★ 4.9 · “Heartbreaking and luminous” — African Literary Quarterly
📙🔥
Teaching with Fire
📚 Pedagogy · Teacher Development

A reflective guide for transformative English educators. Blending practical strategies with philosophical insights for the modern classroom.

★★★★★ 5.0 · “Essential reading” — TESOL Africa

Digital Competency
