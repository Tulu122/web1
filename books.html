My Literary Bookshelf

Words that travel beyond pages — stories and insights from my heart, classroom, and imagination. Each book carries a piece of my soul, written to inspire, heal, and provoke thought.

✨ Published Works

📘🌿
Echoes of the Savanna
⭐ Literary Fiction · African Modern Classic

A lyrical novel that journeys through memory, migration, and the rhythms of rural Kenya. Follow young Adhiambo as she uncovers family secrets tied to the land and its oral traditions. Praised for poetic prose and deep humanity.

★★★★★ 4.8 · “A masterpiece of contemporary African literature” – Daily Nation Review
ISBN: 978-9914-789-01-2
📖🍂
The Grammar of Belonging
🌟 Poetry · Contemporary Verse

An award-winning poetry collection exploring identity, displacement, and the healing power of language. Using English, Luo rhythms, and free verse, Jairus crafts intimate reflections on home, love, and resistance.

★★★★★ 4.9 · “Heartbreaking and luminous” — African Literary Quarterly
ISBN: 978-9914-789-12-8
📙🔥
Teaching with Fire
📚 Pedagogy · Teacher Development

A reflective guide for transformative English educators. Blending practical strategies with philosophical insights, this book reimagines the literature classroom as a space for social change, empathy, and critical dialogue.

★★★★★ 5.0 · “Essential reading for every language teacher” — TESOL Africa
ISBN: 978-9914-789-23-5

“The Unwritten Curriculum”
